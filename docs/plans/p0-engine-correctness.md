# Plan: P0 engine correctness

Source backlog: `TASKS.md`, section "P0 — the numbers are wrong". Ten defects
found by four persona audits on 2026-08-18, none of them caught by the 36
passing tests. The suite proves the TypeScript matches the retired Python in
`legacy/`, and the Python has the same bugs.

Spec authority: KTU B.Tech Regulations 2024. Where the regulation text is not
reproduced in this repo, the Global Constraints below state the assumption the
implementation must encode and how it is made overridable. Do not invent a
regulation number that is not already cited in `engine/constants.ts`.

## Global Constraints

- **Absence is not zero.** A blank mark is `null` and must suppress a
  projection, never feed one. `toOptionalFloat` for anything a student may not
  have yet; `toFloat` only where a default is genuinely meaningful. This is the
  rule the whole engine rests on (`engine/types.ts` header comment).
- **Published beats derived.** A grade published by the university, or an
  internal total published by the college, outranks anything the app computes.
- **No behaviour change without a test.** Every task adds tests to
  `app/src/engine/__tests__/core.test.ts` (or a new file beside it) that would
  fail against the code as it stands today.
- **The parity fixture is pinned to a wrong oracle.** `parity.json` was
  generated by the Python that has these same bugs. Task 1 narrows
  `parity.test.ts` to the expectation fields P0 does not change; no later task
  may re-widen it or regenerate the fixture.
- Run from `app/`: `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx vitest run`. Both must be clean
  before a task is reported DONE. TypeScript is strict with
  `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`.
- Comments explain *why*, in the voice of the surrounding code. No comment may
  describe behaviour the code does not have — a doc comment in
  `state/actions.ts` claiming a merge that was really a wholesale replace is
  exactly how the audit's worst defect stayed hidden.
- Do not touch `legacy/`. It is the retired Python and is not the oracle any
  more.

## Task 1: Fold attendance into CIE (R 7.5.ii)

**The defect.** `engine/cie.ts:38` sums the spec's components. For `TH 40/60`
those are s1 15 + s2 15 + other 10 = 40, which already saturates `cieMax`, so
`computeCie` never calls `attendanceMarks` and attendance changes no
arithmetic anywhere. Two courses at 45% and 90% attendance evaluate
identically. `ui/Home.tsx` totals "N CIE marks lost" from a number that is
displayed and never spent. This is the product's entire thesis.

**Design ruling (controller, 2026-08-18).** The exact per-course-type CIE
split published by KTU for the 2024 scheme is not reproduced anywhere in this
repo, and it must not be invented. So: attendance becomes a **fourth CIE
component worth a fixed 5 marks** (`ATTENDANCE_MARK_MAX`, already the
regulation's number), and the existing components are rescaled
**proportionally** to fill `cieMax - 5`. Relative weights of what is already
modelled are preserved exactly; only the room for attendance is taken out of
them. If the official split later turns out to differ, the change is confined
to the constant table.

**Changes.**

1. `engine/types.ts` — add `attMax: number` to `CourseSpec`.
2. `engine/constants.ts` — every entry in `COURSE_TYPES` gains
   `attMax: ATTENDANCE_MARK_MAX` (5), and its `components` weights are
   rescaled so `sum(weights) + attMax === cieMax` exactly. Add a unit test
   that this invariant holds for all six types; a spec whose parts do not
   total `cieMax` is a silent mis-scaling.
   Worked example for `TH 40/60`: weights 15/15/10 (=40) become
   13.125/13.125/8.75 (=35), plus attMax 5 = 40. Use exact values; do not
   round the weights.
3. `engine/cie.ts` — `computeCie` adds
   `attendanceMarks(<effective attendance %>, spec.attMax) ?? 0` to the
   component sum. `attendanceMarks` already scales to a non-default max.
   The `cie_override` short-circuit is unchanged: a published internal total
   already contains the college's attendance marks and must not have more
   added on top.
4. `engine/cie.ts` / `engine/evaluate.ts` — the attendance percentage
   `computeCie` uses must be the **effective** one (after duty-leave credit),
   i.e. the same figure `evaluate` derives at `evaluate.ts:20-25`, not the raw
   `course.attendance` field. Restructure so the percentage is computed once
   and passed in rather than derived twice in two places that can drift.
   `computeCie(course)` keeps working for existing callers — check them with
   `grep -rn "computeCie" app/src`.

**Tests.** Two `TH 40/60` courses identical but for attendance 45% vs 90%
must produce different `cie`, different `total`, and a different
`needPass.value`. A course at 85%+ earns the full 5. A course with attendance
absent earns 0 attendance marks but its component marks still count. The
`sum(weights) + attMax === cieMax` invariant for all six types.

**Parity.** Narrow `parity.test.ts` so it asserts only the expectation fields
P0 does not change: `cieMax`, `eseMax`, `ese`, `eseCutoff`, `credits`, `plan`,
`attBand`, `target`. Leave `parity.json` byte-identical and add a comment at
the top of `parity.test.ts` saying why the other fields are no longer
asserted and where the replacement coverage lives.

## Task 2: Solve the CGPA goal over the horizon to S8

**The defect.** `engine/goals.ts:23-53` solves a target CGPA as though
graduation were the end of the current semester. At 92 credits and CGPA 7.09,
targeting 8.0 needs 11.99 this semester, so the app reports "out of reach" and
`goalPlan` (`state/store.ts:136`) goes null, which silently removes the
cheapest-route panel — the app's core loop. Over the real horizon the answer
is 9.13: hard, not impossible. The codebase has no notion of a horizon at all.

A graduation CGPA goal and a this-semester SGPA goal are two different goals
and one input was serving both.

**Design ruling (controller, 2026-08-18).** Do not hardcode a programme credit
total; the KTU 2024 total is not cited anywhere in this repo and the
curriculum tables list every elective on offer, so their per-semester sums
(S7 CSE = 74 credits across 24 entries) are not a student's registered load.
Instead derive the horizon from the student's own record:

- `remainingSemesters = max(0, 8 - N)` where N is parsed from the active
  semester name (`"S5"` → 5). A name that does not parse means no horizon.
- `creditsPerSemester` = the mean of the student's own past semester credits
  from `history`, falling back to the current semester's credits, falling back
  to 20 when there is nothing at all.
- `remainingCredits = remainingSemesters * creditsPerSemester`.

**Changes.** `requiredSgpaForCgpa` takes the horizon (a new parameter, or an
options object — the implementer's call, but it must be explicit at the call
site and defaulting to zero must reproduce today's behaviour). It returns:

- `required` — the **average SGPA needed across this semester and every
  remaining one**, i.e. solve
  `target = (pastPoints + s * (credits + remainingCredits)) / (pastCredits + credits + remainingCredits)`.
- `possible: false` only when that average exceeds 10.
- The existing `ceiling` / `slack` / `reason` fields keep their meanings,
  recomputed over the same horizon.
- A new field naming the horizon used (semesters and credits) so the UI can
  state the assumption instead of presenting a derived number as fact.

`state/store.ts` passes the horizon in. `ui/Home.tsx`'s verdict sentence must
say what it now means — "S5 through S8 have to average X" is a different
sentence from "S5 has to deliver X". Keep it one line.

**Tests.** The audit's traced case: 92 past credits at CGPA 7.09, targeting
8.0, active semester S5, ~23 credits/sem → required ≈ 9.13 and
`possible: true`. Same input with the horizon at zero reproduces today's 11.99
and `possible: false`. A student in S8 has no remaining semesters and the
answer collapses to the old one-semester solve.

## Task 3: Missing attendance must not be invented as 100%

**The defect.** `engine/evaluate.ts:20`
`clamp(toFloat(course.attendance, 100), 0, 100)` turns a blank attendance
field into 100% — a full bar, eligible, and (after Task 1) five free CIE
marks. Absence-is-not-zero inverted in the file that enforces it. It also
makes `ui/Home.tsx:59`'s `ev.attMarks === null` guard dead code and the
"No attendance recorded yet" empty state unreachable.

**Changes.**

- `Evaluation.attendance` becomes `number | null`. Use `toOptionalFloat`; the
  duty-leave-adjusted `plan.current` still overrides when raw counts exist.
- `eligible` must not claim a student is eligible on no data. Model the three
  states honestly — the implementer decides between `eligible: boolean | null`
  and a separate `attendanceKnown` flag, but "unknown" must be
  distinguishable from "below 75%" everywhere it is consumed.
- `statusFor` (`evaluate.ts:92`) and `summarise` (`:130`) must not report
  `SHORTAGE`/`DEBARRED`/`lowAttendance` for a course with no attendance data.
- Fix every consumer: `grep -rn "\.attendance\b\|\.eligible\b" app/src`.
  A null attendance renders as a dash, never as 0% and never as 100%.

**Tests.** A course with no attendance field: `attendance === null`,
`attMarks === null`, status `PENDING` (not `SAFE`, not `DEBARRED`), absent
from `lowAttendance`, and its CIE contains no attendance marks.

## Task 4: Unassessed and debarred courses must not be planned as passes

**Two defects, one area.**

1. `engine/goals.ts:71` `courseOptions` ignores `ev.assessed`. An unassessed
   `PBL 60/40` has CIE 0, so a pass needs 50 of a 40-mark ESE → every letter
   impossible → `courseOptions` returns `[]` → `planForSgpa` bails at
   `goals.ts:138` with `reachable: false` for **every** target. One ungraded
   lab kills the whole plan. `evaluate.ts:61` already has this guard, with a
   comment calling the alternative "a straight falsehood".
2. `engine/evaluate.ts:159` and `goals.ts:71` project and plan a debarred
   course (attendance below the condonation floor, `ATTENDANCE_CONDONE`) as
   if the student will sit and pass its exam. The plan instructs a mark in an
   exam they cannot sit.

**Changes.**

- `courseOptions` on an unassessed course returns the full ladder priced from
  an unknown CIE rather than an assumed zero — state the assumption in the
  returned rows or in a flag on them, and make sure `planForSgpa` neither
  bails nor quietly promises a grade that a real CIE could remove.
- A course whose attendance is known and below `ATTENDANCE_CONDONE` is
  excluded from `sgpaProjected` and from `planForSgpa`, and `planForSgpa`'s
  `reason` names it rather than failing anonymously.
- Attendance-unknown (Task 3) is not debarment. Do not exclude it.

**Tests.** A five-course semester with one unassessed `PBL 60/40` still yields
a reachable plan for a modest target. A debarred course appears in neither the
projection nor the plan, and the plan says which course it dropped.

## Task 5: A blank PRJ 100/0 must not be graded F, and a published grade must never read UNREACHABLE

**Two defects in one file.**

1. `engine/evaluate.ts:41` — the `eseMax === 0` branch fires with no data at
   all: total 0 → below `TOTAL_PASS_MARK` → grade F, folded into the
   **confirmed** SGPA. It hits the S1, S7 and S8 presets, which all carry a
   `PRJ 100/0` course. That branch must additionally require `assessed`.
   Note `assessed` is computed at `:61`, below the branch — reorder rather
   than duplicating the expression.
2. `engine/evaluate.ts:100` — `statusFor` tests `!ev.needPass.possible` before
   it checks for a published grade, and a grade-card import carries no CIE. A
   finished `LAB 75/25` with grade A shows `UNREACHABLE`; a published P on
   `TH 40/60` shows `TIGHT`. `summarise` guards this correctly at `:164`
   (`&& ev.grade === null`) — the same rule, implemented twice, one of them
   wrong. Make `statusFor` respect a published grade first.

**Tests.** An untouched `PRJ 100/0` is `PENDING`, has `grade === null`, and
contributes nothing to `sgpaConfirmed`. A `LAB 75/25` with `portal_grade: "A"`
and no CIE is `SAFE`, not `UNREACHABLE`. A `TH 40/60` with `portal_grade: "P"`
is not `TIGHT`.

## Task 6: Store registered credits, not earned credits

**The defect.** KTU computes SGPA over **registered** credits; a failed course
contributes 0 points but its credits stay in the denominator. Three writers
store the earned total instead: `sync/etlab.ts:550`, `state/actions.ts:161`,
`sync/gradecard.ts:136`. A traced S3 card with one F yields CGPA 7.417 where
the truth is 7.15. Every semester containing a backlog is mis-weighted, and
`goals.ts` reads `history[].credits` for the whole CGPA solve.

**Changes.** `SemesterHistory` stores registered credits. Keep earned
separately if a screen wants it (`ui/History.tsx` shows credits), but the
field `cgpaFromSemesters` and `requiredSgpaForCgpa` read must be registered.
Existing saved state carries earned credits under that name — migrate or
tolerate it, and say in the code which was chosen and why. Do not silently
reinterpret old data as if it had always been registered credits.

**Tests.** A semester with one F: registered credits in history, CGPA matches
the hand-traced 7.15 rather than 7.417.

## Task 7: Grades I and W are not F

**The defect.** `engine/grade.ts:21` maps `I` (Incomplete) and `W`
(Withdrawn) to `F`, scoring them 0 against full credits. KTU excludes both
from the denominator until the course is completed.

**Changes.** A third state that drops out of **both** numerator and
denominator — not a grade point of 0. `AB` (absent) is a real fail and stays
mapped to F; only `I` and `W` move. Thread the state through
`normaliseGrade`'s return type, `evaluate`, `summarise`, and
`courseOptions`/`planForSgpa` (a withdrawn course is not plannable).

**Tests.** A semester of four courses where one is `W`: SGPA computed over the
other three's credits only, and the W course excluded from the plan.

## Task 8: Duty-leave credit must grow with classes held

**The defect.** `engine/attendance.ts:24-28` caps credited duty leave at
`held * (DL_CAP_PCT/100)` using **today's** `held`. The cap should track
classes held as the semester runs. Traced: the app tells a student 20 classes
of DL are usable where the truth at that point is 15 — 33% too many, told to
exactly the students who were counting on it.

**Changes.** `creditDutyLeave` and its two callers (`nextAttendanceBand`,
`attendancePlan`) must compute the cap against the `held` figure that applies
to the question being asked. For `attendancePlan`'s "how many can I skip"
branch the denominator grows to `held + skip`; for the "attend n
consecutively" branch it grows to `held + n`. Solve the cap and the target
together rather than crediting DL once up front against a stale `held`.

Also fix `ui/Ledger.tsx:113`: `dlWasted` is a count of classes, printed as
"14.5 days" and rounded to 2dp. It is classes, and it is an integer.

**Tests.** The audit's traced case: 15, not 20. A surplus-side case where the
skip budget shrinks because the DL cap is evaluated against the grown `held`.
